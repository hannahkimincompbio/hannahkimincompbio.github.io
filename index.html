Hannah Kim

I am a Ph.D. Candidate in the Bioinformatics program at Temple University. I am advised by Dr. Sergei L Kosakovsky Pond in the ACME Computational Molecular Evolution Group and Dr. Stephen MacNeil in the Temple Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) Lab. I received my B.S. in Chemistry and M.S. in Computational Biology from Carnegie Mellon University. My research interests include method development, evolutionary genomics, and interdisciplinary HCI.
