Hannah Kim

I am a postdoctoral researcher in Sayaka Miura lab at the University of Mississippi. I received my Ph.D. in Bioinformatics under the supervision of Sergei L Kosakovsky Pond (Bioinformatics) and Stephen MacNeil (Human-Computer Interaction) at Temple University. I received my B.S. in Chemistry and M.S. in Computational Biology from Carnegie Mellon University. My research interests include Bioinformatics, Evolutionary Genomics, and Bioinformatic × HCI. Currently, I am studying tumor evolution in single-cell resolution in Miura lab.
